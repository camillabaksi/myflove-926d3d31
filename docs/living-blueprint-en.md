# Paceable Living Blueprint
Utgåva 2026-10-13 · Your life, at your pace.

A connected description of Paceable’s current web app, its needs framework and next validation steps. Screenshots show existing example data in the demo account on 13 October 2026, not user outcomes. Product screenshots retain their actual Swedish interface.

## 1. Why Paceable exists
Everyday life demands more than remembering a list.

Keeping appointments, prioritising, starting and switching activities can all compete for working memory. When energy and circumstances vary, a static calendar may not be enough to decide what is reasonable today. Paceable is particularly relevant to adults experiencing initiation difficulties and cognitive load, including people with ADHD or other neurodevelopmental conditions. A diagnosis is not a condition for receiving support.

The product hypothesis is that a day becomes easier to understand when activities, time and personally registered capacity can be considered together. The aim is less mental load and more personal control, not maximising completed tasks. This hypothesis needs testing with users; it is not a reported outcome.

Paceable supports personal planning and reflection. It is not a diagnostic service, treatment or objective measure of physiological energy. Earlier MyFlove materials are historical, not evidence of current functionality. This edition replaces older product narratives within the new material package but does not change existing in-app downloads.

## 2. The needs framework: six layers
A map of needs, not a promise of clinical effectiveness.

UNDERSTAND: make the day and personal observations visible. UNLOAD: capture thoughts, times and things otherwise held in memory. PLAN: consider known times and qualitative load alongside registered capacity. DO: find a manageable action and support initiation. REGULATE: offer simpler choices and voluntary recovery. LEARN: reflect cautiously on personal logs.

These layers explain the product’s intent. They are not six clinically validated modules, and not every part of an older vision is a verified feature today. Information should arrive in manageable amounts; detail and Why? explanations can be opened on request. People do not need to complete their history or a programme to receive support.

The earlier research framework uses WHO’s ICF to describe functioning and participation, alongside literature about everyday strategies for adults with ADHD. Evidence for a need is different from evidence for Paceable’s specific solution. Older general research references without a complete bibliography require completion before an external research presentation; they must not become newly substantiated effectiveness claims.

## 3. A calm path through the app
Open → see the day → choose a reasonable next step.

Today brings together a greeting, registered capacity and personal activities. Calendar offers Month, Week and Day. Capture holds what is on your mind; the simpler entry may be labelled Write it down. My space gathers personal support. More opens other areas, including Insights. The older Home remains an older entry point rather than a competing primary journey.

The intended journey is to open, orient, choose something manageable, do it and decide on the next step. This is design intent, not a claim that an automated engine executes the whole sequence. Add distinguishes future plans from things already done. A voluntary energy-impact question can follow a recorded activity.

Paceable projects existing tasks and calendar sources into the same day without creating another parallel account. Fixed appointments and planned items must not move because of an automatic suggestion. The person reviews and approves changes. Missed activities are not moral failures, and recovery does not create extra tasks as a reward.

## 4. Capacity, Load and Your pace
Three different concepts that must remain distinct.

ENERGY is a personal observation. CAPACITY is the registration-based self-report shared by Today, Calendar and My battery. A person can enter 0–100 percent; this is not an objective energy measurement. The latest timestamped energy log or morning entry takes precedence, with an older untimestamped morning entry only as fallback. Zero is a registration; missing registration is unknown.

LOAD reuses the calendar’s explicitly supplied energy markers qualitatively. Unknown impact stays unknown. Gaps and tight transitions require known times; missing duration must not create asserted free time. Future capacity stays unknown until registered. Secondary calculated energy curves remain distinct from personal registration.

YOUR PACE uses Pace: a transient, deterministic interpretation of registered capacity, qualitative load and verified time spacing. It is not AI, a performance score or a medical assessment. Gentle, Steady and Open are guidance where sufficient information exists; insufficient information may mean no level is shown. Open space can stay empty. Gentle is neither a warning nor an automatic action. Low capacity with an empty calendar does not automatically become Open.

## 5. Capture, Calendar and Pace
Help with organising and planning — you decide.

Capture reuses existing text, voice, photo and document capture. Content can be reviewed and corrected before saving. A thought is not automatically an approved task or plan. Import availability and quality depend on the service and input; this screenshot session did not test OCR or recording end to end.

Calendar offers three perspectives over existing sources. Owned editable entries can be planned through existing forms; moving and Undo must preserve the original record’s ownership. Some owned one-off entries support mouse dragging. This is not support for dragging every record type on every phone. Period and family filters change presentation, not permissions or ownership.

Chat with Pace is a separate conversational function for voluntary support and suggestions. It must not be confused with the deterministic Your pace interpretation. Google and Outlook calendar connections reuse personal integrations; provider approval and reconnection may be necessary. No connection, AI action or external calendar change was performed for these materials.

## 6. Personal observations and history
An answer about an activity does not change the battery automatically.

The activity question offers Gave a little energy, Gave energy, Took a little energy, Took energy, Both, No difference and Don’t know. Answering is voluntary. Both and No difference do not imply a loss; Don’t know records no energy factor. An observation links to the original activity occurrence and date, retaining its identifier for retries or corrections.

A confirmed giving or draining observation for today may lead to a labelled small battery-adjustment suggestion. A default five-percentage-point suggestion is an interface rule, not a personal or physiological conversion. It is saved only after explicit approval through the existing check-in. The person can fine-tune the percentage; without registered capacity, the normal check-in is offered.

Energy history can be opened for Today, Yesterday or a selected local date. Impact and strength can be corrected within the same record. Historical observations and corrections do not alter today’s battery. Energy logs, mood check-ins and activity observations carry different meanings even when shown together. Started form drafts and pending observations should survive fresh cloud reads.

## 7. Insights: two complementary charts
Personal answers over time — not causation or forecasting.

Energy impact & mood displays period averages on a 0–100 scale. Energy impact transforms giving/draining observations and their strength: 0 most draining, 50 balanced, 100 most giving. Mood transforms 1–5 answers into 0–100. Neither scale indicates remaining battery. The view has eight weeks or six months; energy answers are read for the latest 60 days, so older periods may lack energy impact.

Battery & activity time compares registered battery check-ins with completed or energy-assessed activity occurrences. Day covers 28 days, Week 12 weeks and Month 12 months within one year of underlying history. Battery is aggregated from registrations; time and occurrence counts are summed. When activity times are missing, the current calculation uses 30 minutes per occurrence. The time is therefore not always measured actual time and must not be marketed as an objective measurement.

A dash or Not checked in means missing information, not zero. Registered zero percent must remain distinguishable from absence. Summaries describe associations in available observations, not causes, diagnoses or future capacity. The charts are complementary detail views. Their newer mobile presentation provides horizontal periods, arrows, keyboard navigation and optional reading explanations; this does not imply that the whole page is fully accessibility-validated.

## 8. My space, recovery and family
Personal support without turning family days into a performance checklist.

My space gathers My battery, Recovery and support for talking about the day. Recovery provides voluntary pause options and further detail. Recovery time is legitimate in itself; the app should not assert a specific capacity gain or automatically fill it with extra tasks. Everyday strategies are support, not treatment.

The family layer shows separate but connected days. Child schedules focus according to time, with untimed items separate, without completion controls or invented energy values for a child. A child’s schedule is context, not the parent’s own load, unless personal presence is explicitly required. Linked parent tasks must not be counted twice.

A linked child account must not gain access to the owner’s private notes, documents, mood or health data through calendar sharing. It reads only permitted profile/schedule fields and explicitly shared items. Whole-household sharing and a selected recipient list mean different things; an empty list means no recipient. These materials show no private child profiles and expanded no sharing or permissions.

## 9. Trust and a connected visual language
Control and continuity must survive a design refresh.

Account-owned local data, sensitive encryption without plaintext fallback and existing cloud sync must be preserved. Sync reads, performs a three-way merge and writes conditionally to avoid silently overwriting another device. Account changes should invalidate stale work and prevent shared-device data leaks. These are safeguards and codebase architecture, not a new security certification.

Private documents use time-limited links. A tab can be reserved at the user’s click to reduce popup blocking, with account-change protection during retrieval. Notifications are optional and use existing consent, quiet mode and links. Publishing the website never installs a new native build. Existing app identities and origin-bound local data must not change as part of renaming materials.

Approved Paceable logos and unchanged stone originals are visual anchors. Current app typography uses Fraunces for orienting headings and Lora for activities, reading and controls. Light/dark presentation, generous reading space and accessible touch targets support calm orientation. Swedish and English exist in the product, but complete translation of every opened detail was not verified here; English materials use honestly labelled Swedish screenshots.

## 10. Business hypothesis and dated evidence
Utility, willingness to pay and retention still need validation.

An initial audience is adults seeking less cognitive load in daily life; family coordination is an adjacent use case. The differentiation hypothesis is the combination of registered capacity, everyday activities and user-controlled small steps rather than another list rewarding more work. This is positioning, not verified competitive superiority.

Earlier Blueprint 6.4 reports 60 registered accounts, including 16 created in the preceding 30 days, read on 4 October 2026. It also reports 681 visitors and 4,616 page views for 5 September–4 October. These figures are reproduced solely as dated history from that file and were not re-read here. Accounts are not active users or paying customers; traffic may include public pages, admin and internal testing.

€250,000 for 18 months is an earlier working assumption, not secured funding or an approved budget. Older ADHD market estimates are not Paceable’s customer base and are not reused as newly verified market figures. Current price, revenue, conversion, retention and willingness to pay lack verified evidence in this delivery. The next business decisions should follow pilot learning, not these screenshots.

## 11. Verified, limited and next steps
A current product view is not a promise of flawless behaviour.

This delivery captures the signed-in web app on 13 October 2026 at 390 × 844 and 1280 × 1000 pixels in light and dark presentation. Screenshots use the existing demo account: no capacity registered today, 65 percent on an earlier day and sparse chart information. No new activities, observations, settings or example records were intentionally saved.

Automatic write attempts were blocked during capture to protect account data, producing a visible sync error in raw screenshots. This is a capture limitation, not evidence of a general product defect or a successful sync test. Standalone chart crops show only real content. Checks were read-only: import, AI actions, document opening, move/Undo, notification delivery and multi-device sync were not exercised end to end here.

Earlier Insights review found remaining large-text limitations around the shared sidebar at 768 pixels/200 percent and bottom navigation. No A2 or B improvement is included in this material task. Physical Android/iOS swiping, system text, VoiceOver/TalkBack, safe areas and notification taps remain unverified. A pilot should examine comprehension, initiation, transitions, plan realism and personal control; no measured medical effects or user outcomes are claimed.
