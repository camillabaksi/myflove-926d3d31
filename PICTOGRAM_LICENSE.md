# Pictogram source and license

The standard pictograms in Family Flow, including healthcare visits and everyday activities, are composed exclusively from **Lucide Icons** and styled by MyFlove for consistent child-facing visual support.

- Source: https://lucide.dev/
- License: ISC License
- Commercial use: permitted
- License text: https://lucide.dev/license

Healthcare pictograms describe neutral places, people, appointments, and actions. They do not represent or infer a diagnosis, condition, or medical assessment.

No ARASAAC, Pinterest, or third-party communication images are bundled. User-supplied photos remain private user content in MyFlove's existing protected child storage and are not part of the standard pictogram library.
